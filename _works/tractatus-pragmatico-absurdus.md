date: 2026-09-10
summary: "A short treatise in seven numbered propositions, in the form of Wittgenstein's Tractatus, on the fear of death from a naturalist and pragmatist standpoint."
where: Paper
tags: [existentialism, ethics, death]
source: "https://philpapers.org/rec/COETPK"
---
<br>

1. The world is everything that resists.
2. A subject is a metastable structure that records the world.
3. Knowing is a finite practice within a semiosis without internal bounds.
4. The fear of death arises from a selected signal, and what it indicates is true.
5.  What is lost is not being, but access.
6.  The absurd is that what has no bounds from inside has an end from outside.
7.  Whereof one will no longer be able to speak, thereof one can speak now.

I am a programmer and I graduated in Philosophy at University of Turin. Disenchanted from the humanities, formal mathematical logic, epistemology and physics are subjects I love. I use a quantitative approach to solve problems and I am very interested in complex systems, information theory and stochastic thermodynamics.

I describe myself as a pragmatist and a scientific liberal naturalist: when people call me a proponent of scientism, I say thank you.

My dream? The F.I.R.E, a quiet life with my wife and to be so good that I cannot be ignored.

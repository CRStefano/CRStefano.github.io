Projects, Work & Talks

The Mind as an Information Filter – 2026

Living systems perceive little of their environment because acquiring information costs free energy needed for survival. This paper introduces a thermodynamic criterion (S-FEEF) proving that compressing perception optimizes metabolic costs against biological viability. – HERE

Typst: From Zero to Hero – 2026

This technical manual explores the transition from traditional typesetting systems to Typst, utilizing its unique integration of markup, scripting, and real-time compilation to redefine document production in academia and beyond. It emphasizes the relevance of bridging intuitive syntax with high-level design in modern document engineering. – HERE

From Arousal to Morpheme – 2025

This paper proposes a biologically grounded model of meaning emergence. By linking arousal-based vocalizations to inferential social roles, I explain how signals evolve into conventional morphemes without prior representation – HERE

Naturalizing Time – 2024

This university seminar analyzed the concept of time from a strictly scientific and naturalistic perspective, defining it with the concept of “level of reality” in a pragmatic and pluralistic way – HERE

Epitaph for Metaphysics, 2023

A (old) Paper for a university exam. In this paper, I will defend Carnap's Deflationism and Contextualism against metaphysical claims – HERE

Analysis of Supererogatory Reasons – 2026

This is my bachelor's thesis and proposes a computational operationalization of epistemic non-necessity through Information Gain. By developing a quantitative criterion based on Shannon entropy, the paper defines an algorithm to identify informational redundancy of reasons within specific contexts of inquiry. – HERE

Intelligence as Adherence to Frameworks – 2026

This paper proposes a formal, context-based theory of intelligence, defining it as pragmatic adherence to operational frameworks and providing a taxonomy to distinguish error from cognitive stupidity. It also challenges the notion of ex nihilo creativity by grounding innovation in pre-existing contextual structures. – HERE

Identity as Information-Preserving Morphism – 2026

This paper introduces a novel framework that redefines identity and persistence using category theory, information theory, and relational ontology. By treating entities as informational nodes rather than fixed substances, it provides precise, mathematical solutions to classic puzzles like the Ship of Theseus, personal identity, and biological turnover.

Enactivism and Predictive Coding, 2024
